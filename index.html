I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Deutsches Institut für Erwachsenenbildung (DIE), affiliated with the DFG-funded META-REP scientific program. My research sits at the intersection of natural language processing and political methodology, with the view that methods should be developed with a strong theoretical underpinning.

My substantive interests span party politics, public policy, public opinion, and meta-science. Methodologically, I work with transformer-based language models, multiverse analysis, and structural equation modelling, among other approaches. More recently, I have been engaging with questions of AI safety and ethics as they relate to social science research.

I hold a PhD in Political Science from the Bremen International Graduate School of Social Sciences (BIGSSS), University of Bremen. I am the author and maintainer of the theoRy package in R.
